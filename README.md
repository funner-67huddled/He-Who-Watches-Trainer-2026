It released on September 2, 2026, and challenges players to explore an ancient labyrinth, manipulate gravity, walk across walls and ceilings, and uncover the secrets surrounding an imprisoned god.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Invulnerability to Falls** — Prevents failed traversal attempts from interrupting exploration when moving across walls, ceilings, and gaps.
- **Gravity State Lock** — Maintains the current gravity orientation while you study puzzle layouts and plan your next move.
- **Unlimited Grapple Uses** — Removes practical limits on the magical arrow's chain, allowing repeated pulls across gaps during experimentation.

### 📌 Puzzle

- **Puzzle Reset Assist** — Provides a quick reset option for environmental arrangements after an unsuccessful block or switch sequence.
- **Switch and Door Interaction Assist** — Makes it easier to test doors, switches, movable blocks, and other bow-based environmental interactions.
- **Hint Progress Assist** — Supports access to the game's playable hint system without altering the underlying puzzle solution.

### 📌 Exploration

- **Secret Path Detection** — Highlights nearby hidden routes and points of interest for players who want to investigate the labyrinth more thoroughly.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Combat Scope** — He Who Watches has no conventional combat system. The bow and arrow are used for solving puzzles and manipulating the environment rather than fighting enemies.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the movement assist and fall protection options when practicing routes across walls, ceilings, and gaps. Gravity State Lock is useful when examining a chamber from a specific orientation. Disable movement-related features before completing a delicate traversal sequence to keep normal positioning and progression behavior intact.

### Tools & Inventory

He Who Watches does not use a conventional inventory-focused progression system. The central tool is a magical bow with a single magical arrow, used to open doors, move blocks, activate switches, and pull the player across gaps. The trainer's arrow-related options support repeated environmental interactions rather than adding items.

### World & Environment

Explore every surface of the ancient labyrinth, including optional routes and areas concealed behind environmental interactions. Secret Path Detection can help reveal nearby points of interest, while Puzzle Reset Assist lets you safely retry block arrangements and chain reactions. Save normally after completing major chambers.

### Combat & Weapons

There is no conventional combat in He Who Watches. The bow is a puzzle-solving instrument, not a weapon. Trainer options should therefore be used for traversal, environmental manipulation, puzzle resets, hints, and exploration rather than combat advantages.
